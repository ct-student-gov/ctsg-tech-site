## Suggested first selection

Start with Sunset Sendoff, Big Red Gala, Cornelloween, and Club Fair, newest first. Each blog-style event entry has a thumbnail, title, date, and short description. Sunset Sendoff uses its poster as one static image. The other three can use roughly 6–10 varied photos, with captions where they explain an activity or setting. A single photo or poster is sufficient for additional events; lack of a full gallery is not a reason to exclude them. Review the Admitted Students Day flyer as the next candidate.
